## 🔴 #1: Performance Max Gains Brand Controls and Search Term Insights
**Source:** Google Ads Blog · Google Ads Team · March 29, 2026
**Category:** Platform Update · **Urgency:** Act Now · **Impact:** 9/10

Google is rolling out two major updates to Performance Max: brand exclusions at the campaign level and a new Search Terms Insight report. Brand exclusions allow advertisers to prevent PMax from showing on branded queries, a long-requested feature. The Search Terms Insight report surfaces aggregated themes of queries driving PMax conversions.

### 💡 What This Means for PPC Teams
Immediately enable brand exclusions on all PMax campaigns where you run separate branded search campaigns — this prevents cannibalisation and double-counting. Check the Search Terms Insights report weekly to find high-value query themes to add as standard search campaigns.

## 🟡 #2: Microsoft Copilot Now Serves Ads Inline — New Placement Available
**Source:** Microsoft Advertising Blog · Microsoft Advertising Team · March 28, 2026
**Category:** AI & Emerging · **Urgency:** Plan Ahead · **Impact:** 8/10

## 🟡 #3: Google Confirms Broad Match Now Uses Real-Time Audience Signals in QS
**Source:** Search Engine Land · Ginny Marvin · March 28, 2026
**Category:** Bidding & Automation · **Urgency:** Plan Ahead · **Impact:** 7/10

Google has confirmed that Quality Score calculations for broad match keywords now incorporate real-time first-party audience signals, including Customer Match lists and remarketing audiences. This change, quietly rolled out in February, means QS can vary significantly for the same keyword depending on audience membership.

### 💡 What This Means for PPC Teams
Audit broad match keywords where QS dropped unexpectedly since February — they may be serving to low-intent audiences. Segment broad match performance by audience to identify which combinations are driving quality traffic before adding negative audiences.
